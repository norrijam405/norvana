## North star

Acre Era is not an infinite catalog and not a generic dropshipping store.

It is a **curated commerce world** where categories, brands, seasons, local markets, and partner programs can become distinct **Eras** without becoming unrelated websites.

Every Era may change its hero media, mood, copy, curation, and data emphasis, while the following remain consistent:

- Acre Era navigation and account/cart shell;
- Acre Era Passport / trust language;
- source and checkout truth;
- Customer Voice;
- archive continuity;
- Watchtower evidence and economics;
- accessibility, performance, and media-rights rules.

The operating doctrine is:

**Breadth is allowed. Garbage is not.**

And the customer promise is:

**Useful things. Fresh finds. Clear reasons.**

## Core customer worlds

- **Current Era** — rotating editorial/drop experience
- **Market** — farm, grocery, local producer, seasonal food
- **Goods** — general ecommerce
- **Partner Finds** — affiliate/referral curation with transparent handoff
- **Electronics** — devices, accessories, authorized distribution, comparisons
- **Fashion** — legitimate brands and wholesale
- **Luxury** — provenance/authentication-heavy premium goods
- **Local** — farms, makers, nearby businesses
- **Archive** — closed Eras preserved as editorial/business history

These are not separate brands. They are different Acres inside one Acre Era system.

## Non-negotiable truth rules

1. An affiliate/referral product must never look like Acre Era owns checkout.
2. Brand imagery/video requires explicit rights evidence.
3. Possessing inventory does not prove brand authorization.
4. Wholesale access does not automatically grant logo/image rights.
5. Local/farm claims require actual source evidence.
6. Luxury authenticity claims require provenance and/or qualified authentication evidence.
7. Watchtower recommendations never silently publish, order, spend, activate suppliers, change prices, or contact partners unless a separately governed ACT lane explicitly grants that authority.
8. Customer-facing unknowns remain unknown; do not invent shipping, warranty, availability, review, provenance, or local claims.
9. Archived Eras should not be silently rewritten to reflect later commercial relationships.
