# Acre Era — Wineries and Breweries: Discovery First (2026-10-27)

Founder wants to include locally owned wineries and craft breweries. This is a future, legally gated category—not authorization for alcohol sales.

## Policy and launch gates
- Begin with editorial research and possible producer profiles, not transactions.
- No alcohol checkout, pricing, affiliate commissions, direct order links, shipping, delivery coordination or payments until ABLE and counsel review the exact model.
- Separate publisher/advertiser, referral intermediary, marketplace collecting payment, and seller-of-record responsibilities. Oklahoma's three-tier alcohol system matters.
- Retail beer, retail wine, spirits and winery direct-shipping permits are distinct authorizations; existing sales tax permits do not authorize beverage sales.
- Confirm Oklahoma state and local licenses, physical premises, marketing/tied-house restrictions, age verification, payment processor rules, licensed shipping, taxes, and other states' laws before commerce.
- Producer content must be sourced lawfully and media rights verified. No unapproved outreach or implied partnership.
- Prioritize qualifying nonalcohol products, verified supplier onboarding, test checkout, and fulfillment for initial revenue.
- Do not apply for a paid liquor license, alter Production, or incur any fees without founder approval.
