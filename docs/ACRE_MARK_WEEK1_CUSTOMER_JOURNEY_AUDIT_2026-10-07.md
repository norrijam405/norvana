# ACRE-MARK-WEEK1 — CUSTOMER JOURNEY AUDIT

Date: 2026-10-07
Mission: ACRE-FOUNDATION-001
Status: COMPLETE / NO IA REDESIGN REQUIRED

## Journey reviewed

DISCOVER -> UNDERSTAND -> TRUST -> BROWSE -> ACT

## Findings

### DISCOVER
Current homepage clearly presents Acre Era as more than a farm marketplace through the Era journey and the Market / Goods / Finds split.

Decision:
KEEP.

### UNDERSTAND
The current global navigation is compact and understandable:
- Market Era
- Goods Era
- Finds Era
- Era Drop
- Past Eras

Decision:
KEEP. Do not add new top-level navigation for branding or SEO.

### TRUST
Strongest current trust mechanisms:
- preview inventory is labeled as preview/not for sale;
- partner pages explicitly say when the partner owns checkout;
- How Acre Era Works explains seller, delivery, returns, and warranty responsibility;
- Market messaging prefers real availability over filler inventory.

Decision:
KEEP and strengthen through metadata/search discoverability, not more homepage copy.

### BROWSE
Goods supports search, department filtering, tags, and sorting.
Partner Finds keeps affiliate/referral inventory separate and truthful.
Market prioritizes local/product context.

Decision:
KEEP. No additional browse abstraction justified yet.

### ACT
CTA language generally matches transaction state:
- preview = unavailable / no fake purchase;
- affiliate = view/buy from partner;
- direct = add to cart;
- local = explore/learn/pilot.

Decision:
KEEP.

## Changes justified by audit

1. Replace remaining placeholder AE branding with selected Concept A production mark.
2. Add search metadata to the existing How Acre Era Works trust page.
3. Include that trust page in the sitemap.
4. Preserve the discreet Admin entry because founder explicitly requested direct Watchtower access; do not style it like a customer CTA.

## Changes explicitly NOT justified

- navigation redesign;
- additional homepage explanation blocks;
- funnel popups;
- fake urgency;
- newsletter interruption modal;
- collapsing Market/Goods/Finds into a generic catalog;
- forcing the logo to become a large hero element.

## Measurement once traffic exists

- homepage -> Market/Goods/Finds click-through;
- how-it-works visits before/after product/partner engagement;
- product detail engagement;
- partner outbound click-through;
- search/filter use;
- empty-state exits;
- first-session confusion reported by founder/users.

## Reusable lesson

The current Acre Era product already communicates breadth through IA and Era storytelling. Brand implementation should increase recognition without increasing cognitive load.
