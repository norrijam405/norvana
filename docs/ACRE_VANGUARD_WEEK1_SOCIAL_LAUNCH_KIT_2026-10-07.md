# Creative rules

Palette:
- Cream #F6F1E7
- Soil #2F3026
- Leaf #526642
- Wheat #E8C68A
- secondary Moss / Clay / Ember only when useful

Typography:
- Schibsted Grotesk: hooks/headlines
- Inter: body/captions
- avoid tiny body copy in social graphics

Logo:
- avatar uses mark only;
- feed graphics may use mark or small lockup;
- do not place a giant logo on every asset.

Photography/video:
- cinematic but real;
- people/products/place should lead;
- avoid generic stock aesthetic when a real story exists;
- never imply a supplier, farm, or product relationship from decorative imagery.

Accessibility:
- maintain readable contrast;
- add captions to video;
- avoid text near UI crop zones;
- use alt text where platforms support it.

Affiliate/commercial content:
- disclose material relationships clearly.
- do not hide affiliate nature inside hashtags only.
