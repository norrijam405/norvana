# ACRE SCOUT AFFILIATE JUDGMENT UPGRADE

Date: 2026-10-08
Mission: ACRE-FOUNDATION-001 / Watchtower Scouts
Evidence source: founder Awin merchant-directory recording

## Trigger

The logged-in Awin publisher directory exposed materially richer and more reliable merchant signals than Awin public marketing pages.

Observed directory fields included:
- conversion rate;
- approval rate;
- EPC / earnings metric;
- payment-status indicator;
- average payment time;
- product-feed availability;
- region;
- joinability;
- mobile optimization and related filters.

Founder explicitly approved adding this judgment method to the Scouts.

## New Scout doctrine

The Affiliate Commerce Scout must use the logged-in publisher directory and official merchant terms as the source of truth.

Public affiliate-network brand examples are discovery hints only.

Each program should be classified:
- DISCOVERED
- JOIN_CANDIDATE
- HOLD
- REJECT

## Evaluation dimensions

1. Exact merchant/program identity.
2. U.S. / target-region fit.
3. Actual joinability.
4. Conversion-rate evidence.
5. Approval-rate evidence.
6. EPC / equivalent earnings signal.
7. Payment status.
8. Average payment delay.
9. Product-feed availability.
10. Deep-link capability.
11. Mobile optimization.
12. Commission and attribution economics.
13. Returns / cancellation reversals.
14. Creative / image / logo rights.
15. Checkout-host integrity.
16. Customer experience.
17. Acre Era category and merchandising fit.

## Negative evidence

Penalize or reject:
- global-only programs presented as if U.S.-available;
- wrong-region advertiser entries;
- poor or uncertain payment behavior;
- excessive payment delay;
- weak conversion/approval performance when enough evidence exists;
- no usable feed/deep-link path when scale requires it;
- merchant experience that would damage Acre Era trust;
- affiliate clutter with little customer value.

## Authority

Scout may observe, compare, score, and recommend.
Scout may NOT:
- join a merchant program;
- accept merchant terms;
- publish affiliate links;
- spend money;
- contact merchants automatically.

Founder remains approval authority for merchant enrollment.
