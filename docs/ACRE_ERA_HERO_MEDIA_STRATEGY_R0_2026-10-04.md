# Acre Era Hero Media Strategy R0

**Date:** 2026-10-04  
**Scope:** customer-facing Acre Era hero presentation

## Objective

Every Era must feel intentional whether or not approved external footage is available.

Hero priority:

1. approved hero video;
2. approved hero image or poster;
3. Acre Era first-party adaptive animated fallback.

No Era should render a blank, generic, or broken hero simply because external media is unavailable.

## Campaign asset minimum

For a fully dressed Era campaign, target:

- 1 primary hero video;
- 1 poster/still fallback;
- 1 mobile-friendly crop or lightweight alternate where practical;
- descriptive alt text;
- internal rights/source record.

Multiple clips are optional. Quality is more important than volume.

## Everyday / department breadth

Hero media is not limited to four product categories.

The four approved visual profiles are presentation engines, not departments:

- organic;
- kinetic;
- cinematic;
- precision.

They may support many customer worlds, including:

- Everyday;
- Family;
- Pets;
- Beauty and personal care;
- Home;
- Market/grocery;
- Fashion;
- Sports/outdoors;
- Electronics/creator;
- Seasonal/gifts;
- Retirement-community oriented collections;
- other future Eras.

An Era selects the visual profile that best matches its mood, not merely its merchandise department.

## Media sourcing order

Prefer:

1. Acre Era-owned footage;
2. authorized partner / farm / supplier footage;
3. properly licensed stock;
4. first-party adaptive fallback.

Do not infer usage rights from public availability.

## Stock usage

Stock footage is appropriate for atmosphere and category context, such as:

- produce being prepared;
- family household scenes;
- pets playing;
- beauty/self-care routines;
- home organization;
- fitness/performance;
- technology/creator workspaces;
- seasonal environments.

Stock must not imply that a depicted person, farm, brand, supplier, product, or facility is the actual seller/partner unless supported by the relationship and media rights.

## Customer-facing rule

Do not expose media-rights workflow, Watchtower, Darwin, IgniAqua, licensing evidence IDs, qualification scores, or internal asset state.

Customers see only the experience and useful shopping information.

## Performance

- autoplay only when muted and appropriate;
- no autoplay audio;
- respect reduced-motion preferences;
- poster fallback required for video whenever available;
- hero must remain usable before video finishes loading;
- mobile bandwidth should be considered;
- critical text must remain HTML, not embedded only in media.

## Initial acquisition target

Do not acquire a huge library.

Start with a small reusable pack across common customer worlds:

- everyday/family/home;
- pets;
- beauty/wellness;
- grocery/farm;
- fashion/performance;
- premium/luxury;
- technology/creator;
- seasonal/holiday.

Target roughly 2-4 strong candidate clips per world for internal qualification, then retain only the best approved assets.

## Long-term direction

As Acre Era gains real partners and original content, replace generic stock with first-party or authorized real-world footage.

The renderer must remain capable of graceful fallback at all times.
