## Operating rule

CJ dashboard browsing is not founder work.
The Scout searches actual supplier product pages, rejects weak candidates, and only escalates a short list.

DISCOVERED != READY_SUPPLIER.

Every candidate below remains DISCOVERED until:
- ship-to-US route is confirmed;
- inventory is known;
- processing time is known;
- delivery window is known;
- shipping cost is known;
- return path is documented;
- image rights are usable;
- provenance / supplier truth is documented;
- total customer price is economically viable.

## Explicit early rejects / deprioritizations

Do not start with:
- drones;
- powered beauty devices;
- medical / therapeutic claim products;
- battery-heavy novelty electronics;
- fragile glassware;
- apparel with complex sizing;
- safety-critical baby products;
- pet shock / ultrasonic behavior devices;
- products requiring exaggerated safety claims.

These are not permanently banned; they are poor first-batch candidates.
