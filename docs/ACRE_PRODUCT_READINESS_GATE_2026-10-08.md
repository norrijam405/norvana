# ACRE PRODUCT READINESS GATE

Date: 2026-10-08
Mission: PRODUCT READINESS / ACRE-FOUNDATION-001

## Purpose

Prevent candidate products from becoming sellable/referrable merely because a Scout found them.

## States

- READY_AFFILIATE
- READY_SUPPLIER
- HOLD
- REJECT

## Affiliate requirements

Before a partner product is READY_AFFILIATE:
- exact source/provider known;
- external seller known;
- partner checkout URL known;
- affiliate network known;
- exact affiliate program known;
- affiliate/commercial authorization documented;
- image rights documented;
- evidence reference preserved.

Returns/warranty summaries are strongly preferred and surfaced as warnings if missing.

## Supplier requirements

Before a direct-supplier product is READY_SUPPLIER:
- exact source/provider known;
- authorization documented;
- image rights documented;
- provenance verified;
- customer total price verified;
- delivery window verified;
- returns path documented;
- stock state known;
- evidence reference preserved.

## Explicit rule

DISCOVERED != READY.

A Scout may discover, score, compare, and recommend products.
Only products passing this gate may be considered for actual storefront activation.

No automatic publishing, ordering, merchant enrollment, or fulfillment authority is granted by this gate.
